Scoring methodology
📋

Symptom match — 60%

Proportion of selected symptoms that align with the disease's known clinical profile.

🔬

Lab correlation — 15%

Temperature, HR, SpO₂, and WBC are checked against disease-specific expected ranges.

🩺

History boost — 10%

Pre-existing conditions like asthma, COPD or hypertension increase score for relevant diseases.

📐

Coverage score — 15%

Rewards diseases that explain most of the patient's symptoms — penalizes over-broad matches.

Important disclaimer: DiagnoAI is built for educational and demonstration purposes only. The scoring model is simplified and does not replicate the complexity of real clinical diagnosis.
